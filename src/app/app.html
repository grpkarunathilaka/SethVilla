EXPLORE MATARA & DOWN SOUTH

Conveniently situated in Walpola, Matara, Seth Villa is the perfect base to explore the scenic south coast of Sri Lanka, renowned for pristine beaches, historic landmarks, and rich wildlife.

Polhena Beach

Just a short drive away, Polhena is famous for its calm coral reef cove, swimming, snorkeling, and the chance to swim alongside giant green sea turtles in their natural habitat.

5 mins drive

Dondra Head Lighthouse

Visit the southernmost point of Sri Lanka and see the tallest lighthouse in the country, surrounded by lush coconut palms and dramatic ocean waves.

15 mins drive

Mirissa Beach

A vibrant hub for travelers, offering white sandy bays, outstanding surf breaks, trendy beach cafes, and world-class blue whale watching tours.

20 mins drive

Historic Matara Forts

Immerse yourself in history with the Portuguese-built Matara Fort and the unique star-shaped Dutch Star Fort located right in the center of Matara town.

8 mins drive
